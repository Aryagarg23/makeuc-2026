GVS tilt control

Press Arm, then hold and drag on the bar: left tilts left, right tilts right, how far = how strong. Let go = 0. Space or the red button stops everything. If the link drops for 1 s the board ramps off by itself. Arrow keys work too.

Slow side-to-side rocking at the max slider's current. Seated only. STOP / Space ends it.
